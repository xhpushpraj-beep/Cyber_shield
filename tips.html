🔐 Cybersecurity Safety Tips

Follow these simple steps to stay safe in the digital world.

TIP 01

🔑 Use Strong Passwords

Create long and unique passwords using uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers and special characters. Avoid using your name or date of birth.

TIP 02

🔐 Enable Two-Factor Authentication

Enable two-factor authentication whenever possible. It provides an additional layer of security to your online accounts.

TIP 03

📱 Never Share OTPs

Never share OTPs, PINs, passwords or verification codes with anyone, even if someone claims to be from your bank or another trusted organization.

TIP 04

🎣 Watch Out for Phishing

Be careful with suspicious emails, messages and links. Attackers may try to trick you into revealing your personal information.

TIP 05

🌐 Check Website URLs

Before entering sensitive information, carefully check the website address. Look for HTTPS and be suspicious of unusual domains.

TIP 06

🔄 Keep Software Updated

Keep your operating system, browser and applications updated. Updates often contain important security fixes.

TIP 07

📶 Be Careful on Public Wi-Fi

Avoid performing sensitive activities such as banking on unknown public Wi-Fi networks.

TIP 08

💾 Keep Backups

Regularly back up important files and documents. Backups can help protect your data from accidental deletion or cyber attacks.

TIP 09

🔒 Lock Your Devices

Always lock your laptop and smartphone when you are away. Use a PIN, password, fingerprint or other available security method.

TIP 10

🪪 Protect Personal Information

Avoid sharing unnecessary personal information online. Be careful about what you post on social media and other public platforms.

⚠️ Think Before You Click

Cybersecurity starts with awareness. Always think carefully before clicking links, downloading files or sharing personal information.
